How to be online on two Discord accounts at once on a Mac

Discord can switch between accounts, but only one is online at a time. If you run a community account and a personal one, switching means missing messages on the other.

A second copy of Discord keeps both online.

Quick answer

Clone Discord with Mitosis, label it (for example Community), and sign in to your second account there. Both copies stay online with their own windows, notifications, and Dock icons.

Step by step

  1. Install Mitosis

    Paste this in Terminal:curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/deadhearth01/Mitosis/main/scripts/install.sh | bashOr use Homebrew: brew install --cask deadhearth01/tap/mitosis. Free; macOS 15 or later on Apple silicon.

  2. Clone Discord

    Click New Clone, choose Discord, and add a label.

  3. Sign in

    Open the copy and sign in with your second account.

  4. Keep both open

    Each copy has its own status, servers, and notification settings.

Good to know

  • Voice works in both copies, but each one picks its microphone and speakers separately.
  • macOS asks each copy for microphone and camera access the first time it needs them.
  • Discord updates itself; Mitosis rebuilds the clone afterwards and keeps you signed in.

Questions

Can I use two Discord accounts at the same time on a Mac?

Yes, with a second copy of the app. Discord's own switcher only shows one account at a time; a Mitosis clone keeps both online.

Does it break Discord's rules?

Mitosis only keeps each account's data separate on your Mac. Using more than one account is up to you and Discord's terms.

How much space does a copy use?

Usually a few megabytes, because the copy shares the original's files on disk.
