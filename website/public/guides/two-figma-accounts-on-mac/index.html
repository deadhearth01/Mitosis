How to use two Figma accounts on one Mac

Figma's desktop app shows one account at a time. Designers who work in a client's Figma organization and their own often switch accounts all day.

A second copy of Figma keeps both accounts signed in, each with its own window and recent files.

Quick answer

Clone Figma with Mitosis, label it Client, and sign in there. Figma (Client) runs next to your normal Figma, each with its own login, data, and Dock icon.

Step by step

  1. Install Mitosis

    Paste this in Terminal:curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/deadhearth01/Mitosis/main/scripts/install.sh | bashOr use Homebrew: brew install --cask deadhearth01/tap/mitosis. Free; macOS 15 or later on Apple silicon.

  2. Clone Figma

    Click New Clone, choose Figma, and add a label like Client.

  3. Sign in

    Open Figma (Client). It starts signed out; sign in with your other account.

Good to know

  • The original Figma keeps its account and settings; the copy never touches them.
  • Figma updates itself; Mitosis rebuilds the copy afterwards and keeps you signed in.
  • Give each copy its own badge letter, emoji, or color so you can tell them apart in the Dock.

Questions

Can I be signed in to two Figma accounts at the same time?

Yes. A Mitosis clone of Figma is a separate app with its own login, so both stay signed in.

Does the clone change my original Figma?

No. The original keeps its account, settings, and data. The clone lives in ~/Applications/Mitosis with its data in ~/Library/Mitosis.
