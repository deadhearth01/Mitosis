How to remove clones and uninstall Mitosis

Mitosis keeps everything in a few known places, and deleting always goes to the Trash, so removing it is simple and reversible.

Quick answer

Delete each clone from Mitosis (choose whether its data goes too), turn off Update clones automatically, quit Mitosis, move Mitosis.app to the Trash, and run rm -f ~/.local/bin/mitosis.

Where things live

Clones: ~/Applications/Mitosis

Clone data (logins, settings, caches): ~/Library/Mitosis/Data

Mitosis settings: ~/Library/Application Support/Mitosis

Background updater: ~/Library/LaunchAgents/com.mitosis-mac.autorefresh.plist, removed when you turn off automatic updates.

Step by step

  1. Delete clones

    Select a clone and choose Delete…: Move to Trash keeps its data, Move to Trash with Data removes both.

  2. Turn off the updater

    In Settings › General, turn off Update clones automatically.

  3. Remove Mitosis

    Quit Mitosis, move it to the Trash, and run rm -f ~/.local/bin/mitosis. With Homebrew: brew uninstall --cask mitosis.

Questions

Does uninstalling Mitosis delete my clones?

No. Clones are normal apps in ~/Applications/Mitosis and keep working; delete them first if you don't want them.

Get Mitosis

Free for macOS 15 or later on Apple silicon. Paste in Terminal:

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/deadhearth01/Mitosis/main/scripts/install.sh | bash

Or with Homebrew: brew install --cask deadhearth01/tap/mitosis
