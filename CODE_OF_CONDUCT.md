# Code of Conduct

Mitosis follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind, assume good intent, welcome newcomers, and keep discussions about the work. Harassment, insults, and personal attacks are not tolerated anywhere in this project — issues, pull requests, discussions, or any other project space.

## Reporting

If someone's behavior concerns you, contact the maintainers privately through [theavni.studio](https://theavni.studio). Reports are handled confidentially, and maintainers may remove comments, lock threads, or ban participants who break these rules.
